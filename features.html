Zero Setup

Install Once. Mods Forever.

Vortex packages every mod and config into a single profile — no jar hunting, no version mismatches. Version updates land automatically in the background, so you always press Play and go straight into the game.

From 1.7 to the latest 1.21 release, pick your Minecraft version and every mod you own follows you instantly. No manual downloads, no conflicting libraries.

Mod Kit

A Toolbox That Actually Helps

Over 65 curated mods and hundreds of toggles — built for the way you actually play. Whether you're clutching a Bedwars final or grinding a Skyblock island, there's a module tuned for it.

Every mod is conflict-checked before release. No more crash walls from two addons fighting over the same file.

  • Keystrokes, CPS & combo counter
  • Crosshair editor with dynamic colors
  • AutoSprint, AutoSneak & reach display
  • Hypixel helpers, SkyBlock & quest overlays
Frame Boost

Turn PC Lag Into Extra Frames

Vortex rewrites how chunks, entities, and particles are drawn, so your GPU does less work per frame. Most players see 2x — or better — on machines that used to chug in crowded lobbies.

Add a performance module like Sodium or OptiFine on top and push it further. Fullbright, optimized chunk loading, and entity culling are one toggle away.

MultiOS Support

Your OS, Our Client

Windows 10+, macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Linux are all first-class citizens. Grab the Windows installer, the macOS DMG, or the Linux AppImage and you're live in under two minutes.
