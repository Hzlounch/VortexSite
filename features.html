Single Install

One Download To Launch

Say goodbye to manually installing and updating your mods. Vortex is an all-in-one modpack that automatically includes and updates all your favorite mods. It supports all the latest versions of Minecraft: Java Edition including 1.21, 1.20, 1.19, 1.18, 1.17, 1.16, 1.12, 1.8, and 1.7.

Vortex updates with every new Minecraft release, so you can jump into the newest versions with all your favorite mods the moment they launch.

Countless Mods

65+ Customizable Mods

Vortex offers over 65 customizable mods and hundreds of settings to enhance your Minecraft experience. Our mods are suited for every type of player, whether you're into PvP, Skyblock, or anything else.

Some of the mods we include are Keystrokes, Coordinates, Freelook, and Toggle Sprint — each crafted to make your gameplay smoother and more enjoyable.

  • Keystrokes & CPS
  • Crosshair Editor
  • ToggleSprint & Sneak
  • Hypixel Addons & SkyBlock
Boosted Frames

More Than 2x Your Frames

Thanks to our talented developers, Vortex offers cutting-edge performance enhancements, often doubling your frame rate — even on low-end PCs. These optimizations are built right into the client, and with optional modules like Sodium or OptiFine you can push performance even further.

Vortex also includes in-game settings like Full Bright, Optimized Chunk Loading, and Entity Rendering to make your Minecraft experience smoother and faster than ever before.

MultiOS Support

Windows, macOS, Linux

Vortex supports all major operating systems — Windows, Linux, and macOS. We offer 64-bit for Windows 10 and above, as well as Intel and Apple Silicon variants for macOS. Linux is packaged as an AppImage supporting Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, Arch, and more.
