Is Vortex Client free?

Yes. Vortex is free to download and play. Optional cosmetics will never affect gameplay.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Vortex is built around modern Minecraft versions. The launcher will show you every version currently available.

Can I use my own mods?

Yes. Vortex keeps your mods organized and makes it easy to switch between your favorite setups.
