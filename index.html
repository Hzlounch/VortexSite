— FAQ

Quick answers to frequent questions.

Didn't find what you needed? Open a ticket in Discord or via the Support page — our team responds within 24 hours.

Is Vortex free to use?

Yes. The launcher, cloud sync, mods, instances and linking are all free. The optional credit store unlocks cosmetics and boosts that never affect gameplay balance.

How do I get the 100 CR welcome bonus?

Install the launcher, open it once, then link your Discord account inside the Credits desk. The welcome bonus is a one-time reward — there are no daily resets, so your economy stays fair.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Every release from 1.8.9 up to the latest snapshot, including mod loaders: Forge, Fabric, NeoForge and Quilt. Mod packs install in one click and auto-update.

Will I keep my worlds & mods?

Absolutely. Vortex stores worlds locally AND offers optional encrypted cloud sync. You can switch computers and keep everything.

How do I report bugs or open a ticket?

Use the Support page here on the website, or go to the Discord and run /ticket with VortexBot. Staff will receive it instantly.
