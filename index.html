Is Vortex Client free?

Yes. Vortex is free to install and play. Cosmetics are optional and will never give an in-game advantage.

Can I use Vortex with my own mods?

Yes. Vortex is designed to keep your favorite mod setups clear and easy to switch between.

The launcher will show the versions currently supported by Vortex when it is released.
