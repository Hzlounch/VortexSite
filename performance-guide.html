Five settings that make every world feel smoother.

A good baseline is better than randomly changing every option. Use the Vortex settings panel to start simple, then adjust one setting at a time for your machine.

Start here

  • Close unnecessary background apps
  • Keep your active profile focused

Then refine

  • Watch frame time, not only FPS
  • Make one change at a time
  • Save a profile for each server
