Maxing Out Your FPS

Follow these simple optimization steps to unlock 500+ FPS in VortexLauncher.

1. Memory Allocation

For optimal gameplay without stutter, set JVM memory allocation between 3GB and 4GB in VortexLauncher settings. Allocating too much RAM can cause garbage collection spikes.

2. Culling & Render Distance

Enable Entity Culling and Smart Chunk Rendering in VortexLauncher settings to eliminate FPS drops in crowded areas.
